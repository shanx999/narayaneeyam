Using this player on your phone

Share this link: Open the Devanagari pilot

  1. Open this link in Safari on iPhone or Chrome on Android.
  2. iPhone: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Android: open the browser menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Open the new home-screen icon, choose a Dasakam, then tap the dark red Save this chapter offline button directly below the selectors. To save everything, open Offline collection below the playback controls.
  4. Keep the app open until it says Saved offline. Test playback once in airplane mode.
