Chapters 1–100: completeness

Review edition · 25 September 2026

What is included

  • 100 distinct audio recordings, Track00 through Track99, totalling about seven hours.
  • 1,034 numbered verses in the supplied scan edition, each included once, with both the original Sanskrit in Malayalam script and the separated-word recital aid.
  • Eight opening prayers before Dasakam 1 Verse 1, matched to the existing recording.
  • Parallel highlighting in both reading forms, with large type, adjustable size and bilingual reciter credits.

Scans and duplicates

The seven new PDF batches contain 158 scan pages covering printed pages 109–260. There are 152 distinct printed pages: the overlaps and a repeated page inside Nar78–85.pdf were consolidated. Two wide scans were read as facing pages so that cropped or repeated material did not create extra verses.

The new pages produced 623 verse appearances, including 23 repeated appearances and two verses already present at the end of Dasakam 42. The resulting 598 new verses extend the approved 436 entries in Chapters 1–42. Dasakam and verse numbers are checked for continuity. Every numbered verse has both reading forms; no rescan is currently required.

The earlier duplicate Nar19–26 2.pdf remains excluded. NAR-pg102.pdf supplies the repaired Dasakam 40 page. The approved Chapters 1–42, including their eight opening prayers, are preserved.

Counts follow the supplied printed edition and recordings: Dasakam 45 has ten verses, Dasakam 65 has nine, and Dasakam 92 has ten. Some online editions or appendices use different counts. The supplied Dasakam 92 Verse 7 is included even though one reference website omits it.

Text and timing review

Both printed paragraphs were ingested. OCR was checked against ancient Sanskrit text references, corrected for missing words, spelling and broken line divisions, and checked against scan images for difficult passages. Printed word separation and chant cadence guide the recital aid. The continuous original is mapped to the same spoken cues without splitting Malayalam conjunct letters.

This is a review edition. Timing is generated by acoustic alignment, checked for omissions and collapsed or inconsistent timings, with targeted realignment. Some very short spoken parts share a highlight. Full listening and scholarly proofreading remain welcome; please include chapter and verse when reporting a correction.

Text-checking references: Narayaneeyam First Step and Sanskrit Documents. Modern translations from these sites have not been copied.

Optional meanings

Malayalam and English explanatory drafts are available for Chapters 1–10. Chapters 11–100 do not yet include meanings; the player labels this clearly.

Phone and offline use

Open the published website in Safari on iPhone or Chrome on Android, add it to the home screen, then open the new icon. Select a chapter and use the dark red Save this chapter offline button. The full audio collection is about 530 MB; keep the app open while downloading. Previously saved chapters are retained and skipped.

Text is cached with the player. Saved audio supports offline seeking. The phone may clear website storage, so check playback in airplane mode before relying on it away from a connection. The computer ZIP is intended to be fully unzipped on a computer; use the website on phones.
