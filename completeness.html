Chapters 1–42: completeness

Review edition · 22 September 2026

What is included

  • 42 complete audio recordings, Track00 through Track41, with distinct file hashes.
  • 436 unique verses in chapter order; each chapter and verse appears once in the menu.
  • All 436 verses have both printed paragraphs available. Overlapping verses at the beginning or end of scan batches were consolidated.
  • The missing page has been supplied: NAR-pg102.pdf completes Dasakam 40, verses 1–4. Both paragraphs were visually proofread against the replacement scan.

Both reading columns now include the printed text for every verse, with parallel audio highlighting. No missing scanned verses remain in Chapters 1–42.

Opening prayers in Dasakam 1

Eight opening prayers from NAr-introverse.pdf (printed page 5) accompany the existing recording before Verse 1, with word highlighting and individual menu entries. The page contains further prayers that are not recited in this recording’s opening; only the matching eight are timed. The single printed form is shown across the reading area. The opening invocation and chapter announcement retain their audio, and Verse 1 begins at its established time around 1:15.

Duplicate handling

Nar19–26 2.pdf is byte-for-byte identical to Nar19–26.pdf and was excluded. Repeated verses in overlapping scans were identified by chapter and verse number and consolidated. Opening and closing material on the recordings is retained. Pages continuing into Chapter 43 were excluded from this edition.

Text and timing review

The established Chapters 1–9 have been preserved. For Chapters 10–42, OCR of the printed original and recital-aid paragraphs was checked against the Sanskrit wording, and transcription errors and missing OCR lines were corrected. Difficult readings were checked on the scan images. The text is a review edition: scholarly proofreading and a complete listening review remain welcome.

Automatic audio alignment was checked for missing, collapsed and inconsistent timings. Three skipped passages were realigned using shorter audio sections. The original text follows the same cues as the recital aid, keeping Malayalam conjunct letters intact; some very short spoken parts share a highlight.

Ancient Sanskrit text references used for checking: Narayaneeyam First Step and Sanskrit Documents. Modern translations from these websites have not been copied.

Optional meanings

Malayalam and English explanatory drafts are available for Chapters 1–10. Chapters 11–42 do not yet include meanings; the player labels this clearly. These drafts are not a scholarly or word-for-word translation.
